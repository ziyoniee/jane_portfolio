The Journey Back to Design

Before I moved to Australia in 2016, I worked as a designer and led a design team in Korea. I came to Australia looking for a new challenge and the chance to experience life somewhere different. Along the way, I learned English, got to know people from all kinds of backgrounds, and was fortunate enough to make Australia my home. That opened the door to a role in food quality assurance.

What surprised me about QA was how much I enjoyed the detective work. I liked going through the details, tracing a problem back to where it started, and figuring out what would stop it from happening again. Over time, I realised I could bring that same curiosity to design by looking closely at how people use something, noticing where they get stuck, and asking why.

That's what drew me to UX/UI. I'm building on what I already know about visual design by developing my skills in user research and testing. This is my next challenge, and I’m excited to bring what I’ve learned in Korea and Australia into the work I do next.
